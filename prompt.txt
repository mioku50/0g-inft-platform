## Root cause
`createZGComputeNetworkBroker()` already returns a broker instance
with a modern plugin API; it **doesn’t expose `.use()`** anymore.
We must register the **tasks** and **inference** plugins in the
supported way *before* we call `.init()`, and then rely on the plugin
APIs (`broker.tasks.createTask`, `broker.inference.getRequestHeaders`,
etc.) everywhere else.
